The venue

Cisano Bergamasco

Concrete, metal and open fire. INDUSTRIAL 14 is a Neapolitan pizzeria, grill house and beer bar in one space, where slow-proofed dough meets flame-grilled meat and beer on tap.

From a quick lunch to dinner with live nights: a contemporary venue built to feel good, no frills. Selected ingredients, open-view cooking, a straight-up atmosphere.
